# MAELVYN — First Commercial Wedge (Hypothesis)

**Decision status:** Candidate for customer discovery; NOT yet validated  
**Review date:** 21 November 2026  
**Working offer name:** Customer Operations Starter

## 1. Why investigate this wedge

Indonesia has an established business-messaging ecosystem. Boston Consulting Group, in partnership with Meta, published a study specifically examining the business-messaging landscape in Indonesia, including drivers, barriers, and implementation practices. This is a credible reason to investigate messaging-led customer operations; it is not proof that a particular small business will pay MAELVYN.

Source: [BCG — 5 Business Messaging Success Factors from Indonesia's Industry Leaders](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2024/indonesi-5-business-messaging-success-factors-from-indonesias-industry-leaders)

The commercial hypothesis is that some small businesses lose leads or spend excessive owner/admin time because customer inquiries are fragmented across WhatsApp, Instagram, website chat, and manual notes. MAELVYN should test whether this pain is frequent, costly, urgent, and funded—not assume it.

## 2. Proposed first offer

**Customer Operations Starter** is a scoped setup service that helps a small business organize incoming inquiries and move them through a visible lead-to-order process.

Initial scope hypothesis:
- map the business's current inquiry channels and customer questions;
- create an approved FAQ / knowledge base and quick-reply library;
- define lead stages, ownership, follow-up rules, and human handoff;
- configure a simple inquiry/order intake and tracking workflow;
- document customer-service SOPs and escalation rules;
- provide a basic weekly report for response time, unresolved inquiries, and lead outcomes;
- optionally configure a website chat/helpdesk tool where the customer already has an appropriate account.

This is initially a productized, human-supervised service. It is not represented as a proprietary CRM, a WhatsApp API provider, or a fully autonomous sales agent.

## 3. Ideal customer profile to test

Prioritize owner-operated Indonesian service businesses or small online sellers that:
- already receive recurring inbound inquiries through messaging or website channels;
- have repeated questions about price, availability, booking, shipping, or order status;
- sometimes have more than one person replying or handing off work;
- can identify missed inquiries, slow responses, or lost follow-ups;
- have an owner/manager who can approve a pilot and measure the result.

Exclude businesses with no meaningful inquiry volume, no named decision-maker, no willingness to share baseline metrics, or expectations of guaranteed sales growth.

## 4. Discovery questions

Interview 15–30 businesses in one chosen subsegment before finalizing the offer:
1. Which channels generate customer inquiries today?
2. Approximately how many inquiries arrive in a normal week?
3. Who replies, and how are inquiries assigned?
4. Which questions repeat most often?
5. How do you record a lead, quote, booking, order, payment, and follow-up?
6. What happens when a message is missed or a staff member is unavailable?
7. Can you identify inquiries that did not become orders because of slow or incomplete follow-up?
8. What does this problem cost in time, lost orders, or customer complaints?
9. What have you already tried, and why did it not solve the problem?
10. Who approves spending on customer operations, and what evidence would justify a paid pilot?

Do not collect customer message histories or personal data during discovery unless there is a defined purpose, permission, and secure handling plan. Aggregate counts and anonymized examples are sufficient for initial research.

## 5. Pilot design

- Duration: 2 weeks after a baseline period is agreed with the customer.
- Start with the customer's existing channels and tools.
- Configure approved FAQs, quick replies, lead stages, handoff, and follow-up reminders.
- Keep outbound campaigns and bulk messaging out of scope.
- Keep any AI reply in supervised/draft mode until knowledge accuracy and escalation behavior are tested.
- Do not require the customer to share passwords; use authorized account invitations or customer-operated setup steps.
- Record baseline and endline metrics using the same definitions.

Metrics:
- median first-response time during agreed business hours;
- percentage of inquiries captured with complete lead details;
- percentage of inquiries with an assigned owner and next action;
- unresolved inquiry backlog and age;
- inquiry-to-booking/order conversion where the customer has reliable records;
- operator time spent on repetitive questions;
- customer complaints, opt-outs, and incorrect answers.

Success targets must be agreed after the baseline is measured. Do not promise a predetermined conversion lift.

## 6. Tidio as an optional component

Official source: [Tidio pricing](https://www.tidio.com/pricing)

The pricing page reviewed on 21 November 2026 showed:
- Free: USD 0/month, 50 billable human conversations per month, up to 10 seats, 100 Flows visitors reached, and 50 one-time Lyro AI conversations.
- Starter: displayed at USD 24.17/month when billed annually, with 100 billable conversations and 50 one-time Lyro conversations.
- Growth: displayed from USD 49.17/month when billed annually.
- Lyro AI Agent add-on: displayed from USD 32.50/month for 50 AI conversations.
- Flows: displayed from USD 24.17/month for 2,000 visitors reached.
- A 7-day trial is offered without a credit card; the account returns to Free unless upgraded.

**Decision:** Tidio is an optional customer-owned helpdesk/chat layer, not a MAELVYN dependency. Its free tier can support a limited test, but the one-time Lyro allocation is not a recurring free AI allowance. Paid plans must be customer-approved and priced separately. Review data processing, retention, access, and channel terms before handling real customer personal data.

## 7. Commercial packaging hypothesis

Do not publish a fixed price yet. Calculate the offer after discovery and a supervised pilot:
- setup hours × internal labor rate;
- any customer-specific software subscription;
- integration and support time;
- onboarding and training;
- revisions and incident allowance;
- tax, payment fees, and a target contribution margin.

Offer structure to test:
- **Setup:** one-time workflow mapping, configuration, SOP, and handover.
- **Care:** optional monthly review, FAQ updates, workflow tuning, and KPI report.
- **Expansion:** additional channels or deeper automation only after the first workflow is stable.

The customer owns their channel accounts and customer records. MAELVYN's reusable templates and generic methods remain MAELVYN assets, while customer-specific content, data, and commissioned outputs follow the signed agreement.

## 8. Go / revise / stop criteria

**Go to a paid pilot** only when:
- at least 5 of the first 15 interviews independently describe the same operational pain;
- at least 3 decision-makers request a scoped proposal or agree to a paid pilot;
- one customer agrees to share a safe baseline and a measurable outcome;
- delivery can be completed without unapproved paid API calls or unverified seller-side integrations;
- expected direct cost and delivery capacity support a positive contribution margin.

**Revise** when pain exists but the buyer, channel, or outcome is inconsistent.

**Stop** when most interviewees do not recognize the pain, no one will pay, delivery depends on risky access, or the unit economics cannot support the work.

These are MAELVYN's proposed experiment thresholds, not industry benchmarks.

## 9. Important limitation

This research establishes a plausible market area, not product-market fit. No customer interviews, signed pilots, paid orders, or measured outcomes have been recorded yet. The next business proof must come from customer conversations and an actual paid pilot—not another feature or research document.
