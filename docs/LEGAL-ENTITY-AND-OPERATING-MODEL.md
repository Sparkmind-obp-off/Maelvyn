# MAELVYN — Legal Entity & Operating Model

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Proposed operating design; legal facts must be verified against current documents  
**Parent authority:** `docs/MASTER-SYSTEM.md`

## 1. Purpose

Separate the MAELVYN ecosystem identity from the legal entity that signs contracts, invoices customers, receives payments, employs people, owns assets, and carries liabilities.

A brand, operating system, venture studio, marketplace, and legal entity are different concepts. They must not be treated as interchangeable.

## 2. Current working assumption

The user has previously identified **PT Waskita Cakrawarti Digital — Perseroan Perorangan** as an existing legal entity with AHU/OSS/NIB and related administration.

This document treats that entity only as a **candidate initial operating company**, subject to verification of:
- Current legal status and eligibility as a Perseroan Perorangan.
- Current deed/registration and AHU data.
- NIB and registered KBLI coverage.
- Risk-based business licensing and operational permits.
- Tax registration and reporting status.
- Whether the actual business activities and contracts fit its registered scope.

No claim is made here that the company already owns MAELVYN, the domain, any venture, any IP, or any participant's assets.

## 3. Recommended staged model

### Stage A — Founder-led operating company

- MAELVYN is the ecosystem/venture-studio brand and internal operating model.
- One existing eligible legal entity may act as the initial operator for approved activities.
- Each venture has a separate internal charter, budget, records, risk register, and commercial owner.
- Contracts and invoices name the actual legal contracting party, not merely the MAELVYN brand.
- Pre-existing brands, domains, code, accounts, and IP remain with their current legal owners unless a documented transfer or license is executed.

### Stage B — Growth company

Consider conversion to a PT Persekutuan Modal when a second shareholder is genuinely admitted, ownership needs to be shared, or the business no longer qualifies for the Perseroan Perorangan regime.

Do not promise or allocate shares through informal messages. Use the required notarial and registry process and written shareholder arrangements.

### Stage C — Venture group

Consider a holding and operating-subsidiary structure only when there is a substantiated need, such as:
- Distinct material liability or regulatory profiles.
- External investors in one venture.
- A genuine IP-holding and licensing arrangement.
- Sale, acquisition, or ring-fencing requirements.
- Scale that justifies separate accounting, directors, compliance, and administration.

A holding company is not required merely because several brands or product lines exist.

## 4. Decision matrix

| Question | Initial default |
|---|---|
| Must MAELVYN immediately be a separate PT? | No |
| Must every venture be a separate PT? | No |
| Can one company operate multiple activities? | Only where its registered scope, permits, contracts, and actual operations allow |
| Does the parent brand automatically own venture IP? | No |
| Does a contributor automatically receive equity? | No |
| Are venture accounts and records mixed? | No; maintain separate internal ledgers and cost centers |
| Is a holding company required now? | Not established |
| Can a future investor enter? | Only after legal structure and rights are deliberately designed |

## 5. Required registers

Maintain:
1. Legal entity register.
2. NIB/KBLI and permit register.
3. Contracting-party register.
4. Brand, domain, software, and IP ownership register.
5. License and assignment register.
6. Bank/payment merchant account register (restricted).
7. Tax and statutory filing calendar.
8. Venture-to-entity operating responsibility map.

Each venture must identify who is the seller, service provider, contracting party, data controller/processor where applicable, and party responsible for customer complaints and refunds.

## 6. When to restructure

Trigger professional legal/accounting review before:
- Adding any shareholder.
- Accepting investment or pooled capital.
- Taking debt or giving guarantees.
- Transferring material IP or assets.
- Entering a regulated business.
- Establishing marketplace collection/split settlement.
- Hiring employees or creating material employment obligations.
- Expanding beyond the applicable UMK/Perseorangan eligibility conditions.
- Creating a holding or subsidiary.

## 7. Required approvals

The MAELVYN system may prepare options and documentation. It cannot itself:
- Create a company or file legal changes without explicit instruction.
- Claim ownership of existing ventures.
- Transfer shares, assets, domains, or IP.
- Sign agreements on behalf of people or entities.
- Represent a proposed partner as an actual shareholder or director.

## 8. Verification checklist

Before public launch, the responsible human and professional advisers should verify:
- Exact legal name and status.
- AHU registration and latest company data.
- NIB and KBLI match to each live activity.
- Required permits and standard certificates.
- Tax and invoice requirements.
- Seller disclosures and consumer terms.
- Privacy/data-processing obligations.
- IP ownership and third-party commercial licenses.

**Operating principle:** one legal operator may be enough for the first stage, but every transaction must disclose the correct legal counterparty and every venture must retain auditable internal separation.
