# MAELVYN — User Journeys & Service Blueprint

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Proposed experience specification

## A. First-time invited participant
1. Receives invitation showing workspace, inviter, role, venture scope, and expiry.
2. Reads what access means, what it does not mean, privacy summary, and decline option.
3. Authenticates through configured identity provider.
4. Reviews role/scope and accepts or declines.
5. Completes short onboarding and lands on a permission-scoped Home view.

Acceptance is not an investment, employment, partnership, IP transfer, or ownership agreement. Declining must be easy and must not trigger pressure.

## B. Opportunity to validated venture
1. Operator records customer problem and target segment.
2. Adds research, competitor evidence, sources, assumptions, and confidence.
3. Defines low-cost validation experiment, success criteria, and stop conditions.
4. Captures interviews or observed behavior with appropriate consent and data minimization.
5. Submits validation summary.
6. Reviewer chooses proceed, revise, park, or reject.
7. If proceeding, operator drafts charter: owner, offer, budget cap, roles, risks, milestones.
8. Required rights-holders review and approve.
9. Venture moves to pilot/build only after all gates are satisfied.

Weak demand, unclear authority, legal risk, or budget uncertainty routes to revise/park/stop.

## C. Joining or contributing to a venture
1. Venture lead defines role and scope before invitation.
2. Invitee sees work expectations, access, compensation assumptions, and end date.
3. Contribution is separately classified: service, loan, license, equipment use, reimbursable expense, capital, or other.
4. Parties agree valuation and rights where relevant.
5. Required agreement is reviewed and executed.
6. Authorized operator records contribution and evidence.
7. Ownership is recorded only where a separate explicit agreement creates it.

No contribution type automatically implies equity. Decline and withdrawal follow the applicable agreement.

## D. Budget and spending approval
1. Operator submits expense proposal with venture, purpose, supplier, amount, currency, timing, and evidence.
2. System checks budget and thresholds.
3. Routes to authorized approvers, excluding conflicts/self-approval where required.
4. Approver reviews impact, evidence, alternatives, and remaining budget.
5. Decision is recorded with reason and timestamp.
6. Approval authorizes defined scope only; it does not execute payment.
7. Payment is performed through an approved process outside the MVP.
8. Evidence is attached and finance operator posts ledger entry.
9. Variance appears in venture reporting.

## E. Monthly venture review
Operator reviews receipts, costs, margin, cash, payables, liabilities, budget variance, delivery, and risks. Finance reconciles evidence. Authorized participants receive scoped reports. Decisions are recorded as scale, hold, restructure, repeat experiment, or exit, with owners and review date.

## F. Offboarding or withdrawal
1. Person requests withdrawal or access is revoked for documented reason.
2. System lists responsibilities, assets, approvals, and handover items.
3. Access is revoked and sessions invalidated.
4. Agreements determine outstanding work, payments, loans, licenses, and ownership.
5. Legally required records are retained with restricted access.
6. Handover and closure are audited.

## Service blueprint
| Stage | User action | System behavior | Human control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Submit opportunity | Store source and assumptions | Reviewer qualifies |
| Validate | Add evidence/test | Track hypothesis and results | Reviewer decides |
| Charter | Define pilot | Validate required fields | Rights-holders approve |
| Participate | Review invitation | Grant scoped access | Invitee consents |
| Operate | Record work/expenses | Check scope and budget | Operator accountable |
| Report | Review performance | Generate scoped views | Finance reconciles |
| Decide | Propose next stage | Show evidence and risks | Approvers decide |
| Exit | Request closure | Revoke access, preserve records | Parties follow agreements |

## Public website journey
Visitor understands what MAELVYN is/is not, reads principles and lifecycle, sees only approved public case studies, and submits a non-binding inquiry. Never publish private participant lists, cap tables, internal opportunities, balances, or unapproved venture claims.
