# MAELVYN — North Star & Product Principles

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Product strategy baseline

## North Star
MAELVYN turns validated opportunities into responsibly operated ventures and productive assets, while preserving each participant's consent, rights, and financial clarity.

The product helps a small group move from idea to evidence, approved pilot, reliable operations, and measured portfolio decisions. It is not primarily a social network, shared wallet, crowdfunding portal, or passive investment marketplace.

## North Star Metric
**Evidence-backed active ventures (EBAV):** number of ventures in Build, Launch, or Operate stages that, within the last 30 days, have:
- a named accountable operator;
- a current approved charter and budget;
- current customer, delivery, financial, or operational evidence;
- no unresolved critical governance or safety gate.

Supporting metrics: time from intake to validation decision; percentage of ventures with current charters/budgets/risks/agreements; paid customer evidence; repeat use; gross margin; cash runway; budget variance; delivery success; approval turnaround; audit completeness.

Do not optimize for signups, ideas, venture count, projected valuation, capital raised, or distributions in isolation.

## Primary users and jobs
| User | Main job |
|---|---|
| Workspace steward | Maintain the operating system, invitations, governance, and boundaries |
| Venture lead/operator | Execute an approved charter and deliver measurable results |
| Contributor/specialist | Understand scope, submit work/evidence, and see agreed rights |
| Finance operator | Maintain budgets, transactions, liabilities, and venture reports |
| Approver/rights-holder | Review reserved matters and record informed decisions |
| Read-only participant | Understand status and records within granted access |

One person may hold multiple roles, but high-risk operations should preserve separation of duties where practical.

## Product promises
1. Capture opportunities without implying commitment.
2. Validate demand before material resource commitments.
3. Create charters with owner, scope, budget, risks, and stop conditions.
4. Invite people into explicit optional roles.
5. Record contributions separately from ownership.
6. Route approvals before reserved actions.
7. Track revenue, costs, liabilities, reserves, and cash per venture.
8. Maintain evidence, documents, and audit history.
9. Support scale, hold, restructure, and exit decisions.

## Non-goals
- No public investment/crowdfunding marketplace or custody of pooled funds.
- No guaranteed-return claims or automatic equity allocation.
- No automatic contract signing or legal partnership creation.
- No replacement for legal, accounting, tax, or compliance advice.
- No automatic absorption of pre-existing ventures, assets, IP, or liabilities.
- No AI approval of spending, debt, contracts, ownership, or distributions.

## Experience principles
Clarity before commitment; explicit consent; project-level truth; evidence over optimism; accountable human decisions; progressive disclosure; reversible experiments; accessible responsive design.

## Product maturity gates
| Stage | Allowed capability |
|---|---|
| Concept workspace | Opportunity notes, draft charters, research; no live money or ownership claims |
| Controlled pilot | Invited users, scoped access, approved pilot records, synthetic/test data |
| Operational workspace | Reviewed agreements, live budgets/ledger, backups, incident process, access review |
| Expanded portfolio | Multiple ventures, consolidated reporting, integrations after review |

## Decision rule
When convenience conflicts with consent, ownership clarity, financial integrity, or safety, preserve the latter and redesign the workflow.
