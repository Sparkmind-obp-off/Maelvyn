# MAELVYN — Brand Strategy & Identity

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Decision date:** 2026-11-24  
**Status:** Approved brand-strategy baseline; visual and legal clearance remain separate workstreams.

## 1. Brand core

| Field | Approved direction |
|---|---|
| Brand name | **MAELVYN** |
| External category | **Venture Studio & Commerce Ecosystem** |
| Platform descriptor | Agentic Shared Venture Studio OS |
| Tagline | **Building Ventures. Creating Value. Growing Together.** |
| Primary color | `#00AFEC` |

The external category explains the business to customers, partners, and the market. The platform descriptor explains the underlying operating system. They are complementary layers, not competing names.

## 2. Brand purpose

Mengubah ide dan peluang menjadi bisnis, produk, layanan, teknologi, dan sistem bernilai ekonomi; lalu menghubungkannya dengan pasar, pelanggan, dan mitra agar tumbuh berkelanjutan.

## 3. Brand vision

Menjadi ekosistem venture dan commerce yang mengembangkan bisnis, produk, teknologi, dan peluang ekonomi secara berkelanjutan melalui kolaborasi manusia, kreativitas, dan AI.

## 4. Core values

- **Human-Centered** — teknologi dan proses harus menghasilkan manfaat nyata bagi manusia.
- **Innovation** — menguji pendekatan baru dengan bukti, bukan sekadar mengikuti tren.
- **Ownership** — tanggung jawab, hak, dan akuntabilitas harus jelas.
- **Integrity** — jujur tentang status, bukti, biaya, risiko, dan keterbatasan.
- **Collaboration** — menghubungkan manusia, bisnis, kreator, penyedia solusi, dan mitra.
- **Sustainable Growth** — pertumbuhan harus sehat secara ekonomi, operasional, dan tata kelola.

## 5. Target audience

1. Founders & Builders.
2. Businesses & Organizations.
3. Customers.
4. Creators.
5. Product Owners.
6. Service Providers.
7. Distribution Partners.

Audience ini adalah kelompok prioritas komunikasi dan pengembangan ekosistem, bukan pernyataan bahwa seluruh kelompok sudah menjadi pengguna atau mitra aktif.

## 6. Brand positioning

**MAELVYN adalah Venture Studio & Commerce Ecosystem yang menghubungkan penciptaan venture, pembangunan solusi, dan pertumbuhan bisnis untuk menciptakan nilai ekonomi nyata.**

MAELVYN menghubungkan:
- Opportunity discovery and validation.
- Venture creation and operations.
- Product, service, technology, and asset development.
- Commerce, distribution, and customer access.
- Human and AI collaboration under clear governance.

MAELVYN is not automatically a holding company, pooled investment fund, public investment marketplace, payment institution, merchant of record, or legal partnership. The actual seller, contracting entity, rights-holder, data role, and fulfillment owner must be identified per venture and transaction.

## 7. Marketing goals

- Brand Awareness.
- Credibility & Trust.
- Venture Acquisition.
- Customer Acquisition.
- Partnership Development.
- Sustainable Revenue.

Marketing must optimize for qualified demand, trust, successful delivery, and repeatable economics—not vanity reach alone.

## 8. Brand personality

Visionary · Innovative · Strategic · Entrepreneurial · Collaborative · Trustworthy.

## 9. Tone of voice

**Core voice:** Clear · Strategic · Confident · Human · Insightful · Credible.

- Modern, premium, professional, and collaborative.
- Bahasa Indonesia for Indonesian-market communication; English for global communication.
- Speak with vision, explain with strategy, and prove through action.
- Prefer clear claims, specific evidence, and direct explanations.
- Avoid exaggerated promises, fake urgency, guaranteed returns, empty superlatives, and jargon without explanation.

## 10. Messaging architecture

**Master message:** Building Ventures. Creating Value. Growing Together.

**Supporting pillars:**
1. Build — turn validated opportunities into ventures and useful solutions.
2. Connect — connect products, services, customers, creators, providers, and channels.
3. Operate — turn ideas into accountable, measurable operations.
4. Grow — improve value through evidence, collaboration, and sustainable economics.

**Proof standard:** every public capability claim must be supported by a live feature, documented service process, verified customer outcome, or explicitly labeled roadmap item.

## 11. Brand architecture and venture boundaries

MAELVYN is the ecosystem-level brand. Ventures may retain independent names, identities, storefronts, accounts, IP, customer relationships, and operating responsibilities. A venture's association with MAELVYN must be described accurately (for example: developed within, operated by, distributed through, or partnered with) and only where that relationship is authorized and documented.

No automatic transfer of ownership, revenue, liabilities, customer data, or IP occurs because a venture appears in a portfolio or marketplace.

## 12. Brand use and governance

- Use **MAELVYN** consistently; do not introduce alternate spellings or legacy candidate names.
- Use the approved tagline exactly, including punctuation.
- Use **Venture Studio & Commerce Ecosystem** as the external category.
- Use **Agentic Shared Venture Studio OS** only as the platform/system descriptor where technical explanation is useful.
- Do not imply all listed ventures are subsidiaries or legally owned by MAELVYN.
- Do not claim trademark registration, domain ownership, social-handle ownership, or global availability without current authoritative verification.
